## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies
This paper is part of a broader research project on proving open problems with AI systems. Codex agents (OpenAI GPT-6.1) proved alpha(S) <= I[f]/n for symmetric f, hence Gamma <= 1.6 I, computed Gamma in closed form for thresholds, and showed Gamma/I -> 1/ln 2 for majority. Claude Opus 5.5 agents (Anthropic), advised by Claude Fable 5.2, proved Theorem A and the bound of Theorem B, wrote the paper and code; other Claude agents independently re-checked proofs and code (not peer review). The author directed and is responsible for the work. The conjecture is Friedgut and Kalai's [FK96]; the level split and first within-level bound are due to O'Donnell, Wright and Zhou [OWZ11, Theorem 4], the level bound to [Shm26, Lemma 2.1], and the constant 2 + 1/ln 2 to [Shm26b].
